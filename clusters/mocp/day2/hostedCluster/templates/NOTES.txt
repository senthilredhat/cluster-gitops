✅ Once the cluster shows AVAILABLE=True:

  1. Extract the kubeconfig:
     oc extract secret/{{ include "hostedcluster.clusterName" . }}-admin-kubeconfig \
       -n {{ .Values.cluster.namespace }} \
       --to=- > /tmp/{{ include "hostedcluster.clusterName" . }}-kubeconfig

  2. Get the kubeadmin password:
     oc extract secret/{{ include "hostedcluster.clusterName" . }}-kubeadmin-password \
       -n {{ .Values.cluster.namespace }} \
       --to=-

  3. Get the ingress LoadBalancer IP:
     oc --kubeconfig=/tmp/{{ include "hostedcluster.clusterName" . }}-kubeconfig \
       get service router-default \
       -n openshift-ingress \
       -o jsonpath='{.status.loadBalancer.ingress[0].ip}{"\n"}'
